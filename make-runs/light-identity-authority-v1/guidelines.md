# OLUK R6 — Light Identity

## Direction

Build one light-mode product and evidence identity with calibrated technical
clarity.

It should feel authored, exact and quietly premium: white porcelain canvas,
cobalt used as a disciplined signal, cool atmosphere around real studio
renders, raised commerce surfaces, and increasingly ruled information as the
user moves into OpenLab evidence.

Media, commerce, transaction, decision and evidence surfaces have different
jobs, but clearly belong to one system.

## What already works

- The three-part header flowing into a free asymmetric hero.
- White canvas with pale cobalt used for structure and atmosphere.
- Real cobalt-bottle studio renders inside cool filled media chambers.
- CategoryBrowse: narrative rail, numbered vertical cards, cobalt hairlines,
  clear labels and restrained color.
- Featured product card: strong vertical hierarchy, structured metrics,
  centered price and clear action.
- OverlapIsland as a product-commerce silhouette.
- PDP: large media field beside a raised purchase/decision surface.
- OpenLab: bold narrative rail beside a dense ruled evidence field.
- Lab Records: search, summary metrics and inspectable record table.
- Thin rules, compact technical labels, strong black headings and generous
  negative space.

## What needs improvement

- Current runtime pages are too flat, raw and placeholder-like.
- Header, hero and product rail need to feel like one opening composition.
- Product cards should inherit the CategoryBrowse discipline without becoming
  category cards.
- Media chambers need stronger optical scale, lighting and grounding.
- Commerce cards need more hierarchy and lift without decorative shadow.
- Price must not overpower product identity, metrics or evidence access.
- PDP secondary media should remain a real gallery, not collapse into weak
  placeholders.
- Commerce-to-OpenLab transitions need more drama, density and purpose.
- Evidence must avoid dashboard-card soup.
- Repeated white boxes need distinct surface roles.

## Creative expectation

Use the references as a rich visual vocabulary. Build the identity immediately
and push it further.

Create options through composition, proportion, overlap, media scale, density
and choreography—not through new themes or color palettes.

Keep one shared identity across complete homepage, product, PDP and OpenLab
contexts. Use real approved studio renders.
