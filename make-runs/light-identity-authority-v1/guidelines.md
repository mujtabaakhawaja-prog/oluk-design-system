# OLUK R6 — Light Identity Creative Direction

## Identity thesis

Build a light-mode product and evidence system with calibrated technical
clarity. It should feel authored, exact and quietly premium: a white porcelain
field, cobalt used as a disciplined signal, cool media atmosphere around real
studio renders, and increasingly ruled information as the user moves from
commerce into OpenLab evidence.

This is one identity, not a set of themes. Media, evidence, transaction and
decision surfaces have distinct visual jobs while clearly belonging to the
same system.

## Visual surface ledger

- The authored page field is white-dominant and spacious.
- Cool ice or pale-cobalt fields create breathing room and section structure.
- Product media lives in cool luminous chambers. The render carries the
  strongest object color; the chamber supports it.
- Commerce and decision content may sit on raised white islands when separation
  helps the choice.
- Evidence surfaces become flatter, denser and more ruled as proof depth grows.
- Narrative and evidence can use asymmetric vertical/horizontal folds. The
  asymmetry must feel balanced, not accidental.
- Section transitions should feel choreographed: showroom, decision surface,
  laboratory threshold, ledger.

## Cobalt law

Cobalt is a signal, not wallpaper.

Use the established Olympus cobalt `#0057FF` for hierarchy, links, selected
states, fine rules, active controls, chart traces and precise moments of
emphasis. Let pale cobalt atmosphere organize space without tinting everything.
The system should remain recognizably cobalt even when very little saturated
blue is visible.

Cobalt hairlines can give white cards edge definition. Evidence rules may be
cool neutral when constant cobalt would become noisy. Do not introduce gold,
purple gradients, dark Majestic shells, or a second brand accent.

## Product truth

Do not invent facts or claims.

- MK-2866 / Ostarine / 15 mg / 90 servings / SARM / capsules.
- RAD-140 / 8 mg / 60 servings / SARM / capsules.
- MK-677 / Ibutamoren / 15 mg / 90 servings / GH secretagogue / capsules.
- `servings` and `capsules` are different concepts and must never be silently
  substituted.
- Use `>99%` only where the supplied reference explicitly governs that value.
- Use `£43.00` only as the supplied MK-2866 fixture, not as a universal price.
- Laboratory methods, batch statuses, testing coverage, purity averages,
  performance benefits and medical outcomes must remain clearly labelled
  placeholders unless supplied as governed product data.

## Weakness brief

Attack these weaknesses in the existing material:

- Product cards can collapse into generic ecommerce tiles.
- Header, menu and hero can feel like separate stacked bands instead of one
  asymmetric opening composition.
- Category cards need identity without decorative theme drift.
- Media chambers sometimes lose the fidelity and lighting quality of the studio
  renders.
- PDP media and commerce content need a decisive vertical/horizontal split.
- Price can overpower product identity, metrics and evidence entry points.
- Commerce-to-evidence transitions are often abrupt or missing.
- OpenLab density can become either a generic dashboard or a collection of
  floating mini-cards.
- Repeated cards, chips and shadows flatten the hierarchy instead of clarifying
  it.

## Creative expectations

Build the system in real context. Show complete sections and consecutive page
compositions, not an inventory of raw rectangles.

Preserve the strongest existing ideas, then push them:

- Let the three-part header flow into a free, asymmetric hero.
- Carry the CategoryFamilyRail's vertical editorial contract into product
  commerce without making category and product cards identical.
- Use real approved studio renders inside media chambers.
- Explore an OverlapIsland commerce silhouette, a ruled chamber silhouette and
  a featured rail silhouette while keeping one shared identity.
- Keep prices calm and centered where that strengthens the composition.
- Make the PDP feel like a product monograph whose media, transaction and
  evidence regions each earn their space.
- Make the OpenLab threshold feel like crossing from a showroom into a
  laboratory, without becoming theatrical.
- Surprise through spatial hierarchy, cropping, scale, controlled overlap,
  rhythm and information choreography—not through unrelated palettes.

Provide controlled alternatives only where the spatial decision remains open.
Alternatives must share the same surface law, cobalt discipline, media fidelity
and typographic voice. Do not present three unrelated agencies or select a
champion on our behalf.

## Constraints

- Light mode only.
- Use current-file R6 references only.
- Use real approved studio renders; do not replace them with linework boxes.
- Keep customer-facing language factual and restrained.
- No decorative drop-shadow language. A restrained functional lift is allowed
  on raised commerce or decision islands; evidence surfaces stay predominantly
  ruled and flat.
- No screenshot tracing and no direct logo harvesting.
- Keep the output editable and reusable in Figma.
- Do not solve runtime, SSR, routing, React, Tailwind, TypeScript, breakpoints,
  telemetry or production state management in this run.
