# Human Review Gate

Make does not score, rank or promote its own work.

The foundation must pass this gate before any completion packet or runtime work.

## Gate A — Cohesive identity

- Do Homepage, PDP and OpenLab read as the same authored light system?
- Are media, transaction, decision and evidence surfaces visibly distinct
  without feeling like separate brands?
- Does cobalt behave as a controlled signal rather than a page tint?
- Is the white/cool-field rhythm deliberate across consecutive sections?

## Gate B — Structural improvement

- Does the three-part header become one opening composition with the hero?
- Is asymmetry balanced at both section and page level?
- Is CategoryBrowse resolved without six decorative theme cards?
- Do product card options differ in silhouette and information choreography?
- Does the PDP feel like a product monograph?
- Does the OpenLab threshold become denser, ruled and inspectable?

## Gate C — Fidelity and truth

- Are approved studio renders preserved at useful scale and quality?
- Are media chambers cool and supportive rather than grey or over-saturated?
- Are price and purchase actions proportioned against product identity and
  evidence?
- Are servings and capsules kept distinct?
- Are purity, testing, batch, medical and performance claims governed or
  explicitly marked as placeholders?

## Gate D — Useful options

- Do options share one identity?
- Are differences spatial, compositional and choreographic?
- Is each option viable enough to compare?
- Are unresolved decisions labelled without Make selecting a champion?

## Rejection conditions

Reject or correct the run if it:

- produces three color themes;
- returns mostly raw primitives or annotation boards;
- uses a dark/gold Majestic shell;
- removes real studio renders in favor of empty chambers;
- repeats floating mini-cards for every kind of information;
- treats `461:95165` as a QuickAdd dimension authority;
- invents factual claims;
- includes React, SSR, routing or implementation architecture;
- declares its own winner.

## Lock statement

After review, record exactly one of:

- `LOCK LIGHT IDENTITY AUTHORITY V1 — foundation accepted; listed modulations
  remain open.`
- `LOCK LIGHT IDENTITY AUTHORITY V1 — foundation and modulation [name]
  accepted.`
- `REVISE LIGHT IDENTITY AUTHORITY V1 — correct only: [specific defects].`
