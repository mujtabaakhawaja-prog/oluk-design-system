## Authority statement

This document combines the four supplied viewpoints into one controlling plan. The legacy 00–10 outline remains useful as a list of artifact families, but the canonical dependency model is:

> **authority triage → brand/domain truth → native directional baseline → PNG-led Figma Make creative frontier → selective native integration → cross-route Make design selection → foundation extraction → system validation → foundation promotion → adaptive components and domain compositions → complete experience proof → runtime verification → governed evolution**

The active project does not restart from blank-slate brand discovery. Brand/domain truth, product grammar and much of the functional composition logic remain useful. Human review has, however, rejected the inherited material treatment. The program therefore reopens only the creative-frontier and authority-composition work needed to replace that treatment before foundation promotion resumes. Runtime proofs are corrective evidence, not automatic authority.

1. **Identity must precede tokens.** Correct as a dependency rule.

Make now uses those proofs as creative direction, develops stronger customer-facing designs, then introduces selected native nodes and variables after the visual frontier has produced a champion.

## Locked identity laws

- Cobalt belongs to the product vessel first; interface blue is borrowed as controlled signal, action, and instrumentation.
- The ambient light-mode canvas is cool blue-white, never cream, beige, ivory or muddy neutral grey.
- The canvas remains visibly continuous around modules. No media, commerce, evidence or navigation section may masquerade as a full-width page-background fill.
- White product-label stock remains the highest-authority white object; true `#FFFFFF` is also valid for deliberately raised decision, commerce and information planes when the cool canvas and material separation keep the object hierarchy clear.
- Default ink is deep blue-biased navy, not pure black.
- Commerce is a calm raised foreground; evidence is systematic; media carries product-led atmosphere; all three may lift from the canvas through controlled material depth rather than flat tonal bands.
- `#0057FF` is the core identity/metric signal and `#256DFF` is the current interactive cobalt; neither is default blue body text or global wallpaper.
- `#E4E5F1` is structural mark material, not the dominant canvas.
- The PDP hero is the deliberate exception to bounded media-chamber grammar: one continuous atmospheric rail extends beyond the 1440 content width, carries the approved transparent bottle directly, and receives one raised purchase plane on the same field. It has no left media card, outer border or cobalt top-edge rule.
- Outside that PDP hero exception, luminous linear and full-field media chambers are authored raised planes inside larger rails. Their gradient fills the chamber interior, never the surrounding section or page canvas. They may carry product media or visualized information and use restrained blue-white gradients rather than grey placeholder fills.
- Light-mode elevation is permitted and expected where it clarifies material hierarchy. Candidate shadows are blue-grey, soft and low-opacity; elevation must be systemic, not arbitrary decoration.
- Plane elevation and product-object shadows are separate responsibilities. A raised card/chamber uses restrained ambient depth; a bottle may use a localized cobalt float and neutral ground shadow without turning the containing surface into a blue glow.
- Sections, rails and multi-column containers default to raised white or optically white planes, not blue/grey-filled bands. A single cobalt top-edge hairline is authorized only when it expresses one continuous section or multi-cell rail relationship; it is not the default accent for cards, heroes, every cell or a four-sided perimeter.
- Broad navy/black inverse bands are prohibited in light mode. Dark-object product testing remains required, but `surface/inverse`, `text/inverse` and `border/inverse` are quarantined utilities rather than pacing devices.
- Evidence, metric, specification, batch, and classification relationships are first-class identity grammar.
- Open editorial space and compressed technical density form a deliberate rhythm.
- Plus Jakarta Sans carries primary identity and display hierarchy. Inter is the only secondary/supporting family. Barlow Condensed, Cousine and other inherited technical fonts are rejected.
- Mature runtime/design capabilities may be retained during Phase 04 when they preserve an accepted semantic job. They must be rebound, optically corrected and proven as native authority assets; they are not automatically promoted as the Phase 08 production library.

# Canonical phase runbooks

Every phase uses the same control schema: purpose, entry, mandatory build, verification, prohibition, exit authority, and reopen rule.

## Phase 00 — Authority Intake and Triage

**Purpose:** Determine what may legitimately constrain the new system.

**Entry:** A new program, major redesign, inherited corpus, or disputed authority state.

**Mandatory build:** Workspace charter; source ledger; authority classes; provenance and truth registry; supersession map; contradiction register; exclusions/quarantine; phase-readiness map.

**Execution:** Inventory every source; classify it as current truth, hard constraint, candidate, inspiration, diagnostic evidence, superseded, or excluded; resolve contradictions without counting repetition as proof.

**Verification:** Every inherited claim has source, status, owner or explicit ownership gap, and downstream eligibility.

**Prohibited:** Token creation, component extraction, winner selection, or importing “production-ready” status from legacy labels.

**Exit authority:** The program knows what is current, what is only evidence, and which later phases are already materially satisfied.

**Reopen when:** A new source changes truth, provenance, ownership, or supersession.

## Phase 01 — Brand and Domain Truth

**Purpose:** Define the meaning and information relationships the identity must preserve.

**Entry:** Phase 00 source classification complete.

**Mandatory build:** Brand thesis; audience/positioning; emotional and functional axes; fixed-versus-variable ledger; product/domain taxonomy; naming, metric, specification, evidence, batch and classification grammar; controlled exploration input kit; missing-truth rules.

**Execution:** Separate factual/domain truth from current visual treatments. Record what must remain true even if typography, color, layout, or media direction changes.

**Verification:** Stakeholders can identify immutable relationships without referring to old component anatomy.

**Prohibited:** Treating an old UI, token set, or page layout as brand truth.

**Exit authority:** Meaning, facts, hierarchy and relationships are stable enough for equal-fidelity identity exploration.

**Reopen when:** Product truth, regulated copy, taxonomy, evidence relationships, or positioning changes.

## Phase 02 — Creative Frontier

**Purpose:** Discover materially different identity systems before premature convergence.

**Entry:** Truth and common proof content are controlled; representative assets are available or honestly marked missing.

**Mandatory build:** Three to five architecturally distinct directions for a full frontier, or three equal-fidelity targeted material systems for a corrective reopen; identical proof content; equal-fidelity typography, image, composition, scale, density, material, color and motion studies; evaluation matrix; preserved alternatives.

**Execution:** Build different visual logics rather than palette swaps. Compare each direction across narrative, object/media, transaction and dense-information roles. For the active corrective reopen, every candidate must preserve visible cool canvas around raised planes, use independently elevated linear/full-field chambers outside the PDP hero, apply the continuous atmospheric-rail exception inside the PDP hero, and use white rails with cobalt top-edge hairlines only where authorized. Cream/beige/ivory, broad dark bands, page-wide chromatic section fills, dashboard-like grey card stacks and decorative inversion are excluded.

**Verification:** Candidate differences remain visible in greyscale and at reduced zoom; no candidate receives privileged fidelity. Every candidate passes a containment check: canvas, chamber, raised information plane and rail remain visually separable without any module becoming the page background.

**Prohibited:** Predetermined champion naming, token extraction, or using runtime feasibility to eliminate creative directions prematurely.

**Exit authority:** Explicit human champion selection with rationale and rejected-direction archive.

**Reopen when:** Phase 04 proves the selected direction cannot survive a required functional role.

## Phase 03 — Direction Lock and Master Asset System

**Purpose:** Create the governed visual source material every downstream proof may use.

**Entry:** Champion direction selected.

**Mandatory build:** Decision record; logo/mark system; packaging/object masters; render, photography, illustration, material and lighting rules; master and compatibility assets; asset status matrix; canonical proof-asset set; allowed-use and supersession metadata.

**Execution:** Register each asset as master, approved, compatibility, candidate, missing, deprecated, or rejected. Keep exploration inputs separate from post-champion masters.

**Verification:** Every downstream asset has source, version, owner, status and allowed uses; missing inputs remain visible.

**Prohibited:** Fabricating a missing master, promoting a screenshot crop by implication, or assuming assets are permanently immutable.

**Exit authority:** Required proof compositions can be built without searching legacy projects or substituting ungoverned imagery.

**Reopen when:** A master changes, a required usage is unsupported, or asset stress reveals a compatibility failure.

## Phase 04 — Identity Authority Compositions

**Purpose:** Prove the selected identity across materially different jobs before extracting a reusable system.

**Entry:** Champion and sufficient governed/compatibility assets exist.

**Mandatory build:** Narrative/editorial proof; media-led proof; transaction/action proof; dense information/evidence proof; material/elevation proof; relevant form/flow proof; long-form rhythm; early compact recomposition where hierarchy changes. An inverse proof is optional and requires a separately authorized product need; it is not a light-mode pacing requirement.

**Execution:** Use real content relationships and real assets. Resolve composition, density, hierarchy and material behavior at both detail and page scale. Prove the relationship `visible cool canvas → bounded elevated luminous chamber → independently raised white decision/information plane`, including linear and chamber-internal full-field variants plus white rails with cobalt top-edge hairlines.

**Verification:** The same identity survives open editorial space, product-led media, decisive commerce and compressed evidence without becoming separate dialects; no proof depends on cream ambience, flat grey placeholders, full-width colored section fills, broad dark slabs or ungoverned shadow styling. Canvas must remain visible around raised modules.

**Prohibited:** Locking token values, building a component library, or mistaking annotation chrome for customer UI.

**Exit authority — Identity Gate:** Human accepts the direction across roles; source boards are locked and versioned.

**Reopen when:** A mandatory role fails or a new asset class contradicts the direction.

## Phase 05 — Candidate Foundation Model

**Purpose:** Extract the smallest reusable rule set that explains the accepted compositions.

**Entry:** Identity Gate passed; authority compositions and source assets are identified.

**Mandatory build:** Primitive and semantic candidates; typography roles/styles; density and spacing; grid/container/media-ratio/section rhythm; shape/radius/stroke; opacity/material/elevation; linear/full-field chamber recipes; focus/selection; motion; icon construction; temporary evaluation modes; scopes, descriptions and code syntax.

**Execution:** Build primitives and semantics together. Keep semantic names stable while mappings remain provisional. Bind candidate working copies, never the locked source boards. Because Figma gradient paints cannot be governed through the same solid-paint variable binding path, chamber gradients and shadows remain explicitly versioned material recipes/effect or paint styles until validated; do not disguise them as ordinary color tokens.

**Verification:** One semantic API can drive the authority roles; raw values exist only in explicitly marked experiments and authoring chrome.

**Prohibited:** Component aliases, route-specific tokens, production publication, dark-theme pairing, or runtime export before proof convergence.

**Exit authority:** Complete candidate foundation model ready for stress testing.

**Reopen when:** Phase 06 reveals a missing role or foundation rule; correct narrowly and rerun affected proofs.

## Phase 06 — System Validation and Optical Correction

**Purpose:** Prove that the extracted system reproduces and strengthens the identity under stress.

**Entry:** Candidate foundations can drive all authority working copies.

**Mandatory build:** Corrected authority remaps; surface/material/elevation hierarchy; linear/full-field chamber tests; light- and dark-object tests; typography/contrast matrix; interaction/state matrix; dense/low-density tests; content extremes; responsive recompositions; accessibility simulations; optical corrections; exception and prohibited-pairing register; comparative candidate scorecard.

**Execution:** Change modes only at parent frames; prohibit manual recoloring. Route every failure to asset, identity, foundation, content/data, accessibility or responsive ownership.

**Verification:** Representative assets, states, content extremes and viewports remain coherent without local overrides or raw-value drift.

**Prohibited:** Selecting the preferred mode by taste alone, renaming a mode `Light`, or beginning broad component extraction.

**Exit authority — System Gate:** Human selects the winning mappings after all blocking tests pass; accepted exceptions are explicit.

**Reopen when:** A downstream component or experience reveals a genuine missing semantic role; rerun only invalidated proofs.

## Phase 07 — Foundation Authority and Layout Grammar

**Purpose:** Promote only proven rules into a stable consumable foundation API.

**Entry:** System Gate passed with exact candidate/version and human decision record.

**Mandatory build:** Promoted variable collections/styles; production `Light` mode; archived rejected modes; typography and non-color foundations; final layout/responsive grammar; ownership/version/lifecycle metadata; change log; migration/deprecation notes.

**Execution:** Freeze accepted names and mappings for the version. Publish only the allowlisted foundation authority; separate experiments and archives.

**Verification:** No unresolved aliases, duplicate names, raw values, ambiguous scopes, missing descriptions, or unsupported responsive rules.

**Prohibited:** Retaining evaluation modes as accidental production themes or publishing speculative component aliases.

**Exit authority:** Stable foundation version is eligible for component consumption.

**Reopen when:** A validated downstream need cannot be expressed; version and migrate rather than silently editing authority.

## Phase 08 — Component Contracts and Primitives

**Purpose:** Define reusable semantic units from accepted repetition.

**Entry:** Foundation Authority published and component candidate appears repeatedly across approved proofs.

**Mandatory build:** Component registry; semantic responsibility; anatomy; content/data requirements; honest fallbacks; variants/sizes/states; interaction/motion/accessibility/responsive contracts; source/owner/consumer/dependency/lifecycle metadata; anatomy and API documentation.

**Execution:** Extract atoms before molecules; build one component family at a time; bind all applicable properties to foundations; validate default, hover, focus, selected, disabled, loading and status behavior.

**Verification:** Variant counts are intentional, content extremes work, focus/touch/contrast pass, and no component contains raw styling or page-specific narrative.

**Prohibited:** Speculative inventory, page fragments disguised as components, universal “everything components,” or variant explosion.

**Exit authority:** Component contracts represent proven recurring semantic jobs and can be adopted by modules.

**Reopen when:** Cross-context use exposes a contract failure; fix component or narrowly reopen foundations with impact record.

## Phase 09 — Domain Modules and Composition Library

**Purpose:** Assemble components into recurring, authored, domain-specific structures.

**Entry:** Required primitive component contracts are approved.

**Mandatory build:** Domain component families; capability ownership; recurring modules/sections; slot/dependency/state/fallback/density/responsive behavior; composition registry; adoption map; cross-context comparison proofs.

**Execution:** Modules own capabilities; pages own sequence and emphasis. Preserve context projections instead of forcing one universal anatomy.

**Verification:** A module works in every declared context without new local styling or content ownership ambiguity.

**Prohibited:** Building entire pages as components, predeclaring every future section, or erasing context in the name of reuse.

**Exit authority:** Recurring compositions are stable enough for complete experience assembly.

**Reopen when:** Page/flow proof exposes missing state, slot, fallback or responsive behavior.

## Phase 10 — Experience, Page and Flow Proofs

**Purpose:** Prove the system as complete customer experiences.

**Entry:** Required modules and component contracts exist.

**Mandatory build:** Representative complete pages; connected flows and lifecycle states; desktop and compact recompositions; empty/error/loading/unavailable/recovery paths; content extremes; accessibility evidence; drift audit against identity and foundations.

**Execution:** Build representative families before exhaustive coverage. Preserve relationship hierarchy rather than scaling desktop frames.

**Verification:** Identity, semantics, states and responsive priorities remain coherent across the full experience without route-local dialects.

**Prohibited:** Runtime implementation as a substitute for design proof or silent page-local overrides.

**Exit authority — Experience Gate:** Human approves the representative experience set and exact design version for translation.

**Reopen when:** A flow exposes component/module shortcomings; route findings to the narrow owner and rerun affected experiences.

## Phase 11 — Runtime Translation and Independent Verification

**Purpose:** Implement the approved design authority without changing its meaning.

**Entry:** Experience Gate passed; exact design version, governed assets and fixtures identified.

**Mandatory build:** Design-to-runtime token/style map; component mappings; governed fixture and asset allowlist; visual parity and interaction evidence; accessibility/performance/functional tests; implementation status; reversible rollout and rollback plan.

**Execution:** Translate rather than redesign. Track designed, mapped, implemented, reviewed, verified and released independently.

**Verification:** Independent review confirms parity, behavior, accessibility and performance against the exact approved version.

**Prohibited:** Allowing implementation convenience to silently redefine visual authority or presenting a runtime capture as normative design truth.

**Exit authority:** Verified implementation candidate is eligible for release promotion.

**Reopen when:** Translation reveals an impossible contract; correct mapping first, then reopen design only with evidence and impact analysis.

## Phase 12 — Publication, Adoption and Evolution

**Purpose:** Make the released system understandable, adoptable, reversible and governable.

**Entry:** Runtime candidate independently verified; release authority identified.

**Mandatory build:** Published library and downstream allowlist; release notes; adoption/migration guide; contribution model; deprecation/supersession process; regression/drift audits; design-debt register; archive; exact human promotion record; rollback path.

**Execution:** Promote the exact candidate/version, communicate changes, monitor adoption and preserve rejected/superseded evidence.

**Verification:** Consumers can identify current authority, eligibility, changes, deprecated items, migration path and rollback procedure.

**Prohibited:** One undifferentiated “complete” status, deletion of decision history, or unversioned edits to published authority.

**Exit authority:** The released system can evolve without losing provenance or silently drifting.

**Reopen when:** New truth, product needs, regressions or debt initiate a classified change request routed to the responsible phase.

### Controlled feedback loops

- Phase 04 may reopen Phase 02–03 when the identity or assets fail a real job.
- Explicit human rejection of a material or composition law reopens the smallest responsible Phase 02/04 lane immediately; downstream mechanical correctness cannot overrule that rejection.
- Phase 06 may remap Phase 05 candidates without reopening the identity direction.
- Phase 08–09 may add a narrowly justified semantic role, followed by Phase 06 revalidation.
- Phase 10 may reopen component or composition contracts.
- Phase 11 may correct implementation mappings but cannot silently redefine visual authority.

| Accessibility/responsive/content failure | Phase 06 | Failed proof family and dependent promotion record |
| Component contract failure | Phase 08 | Consuming modules and experience proofs |
| Module ownership/slot failure | Phase 09 | Consuming page/flow proofs |
| Experience-flow failure | Phase 10 | Relevant component/module contracts and translation eligibility |
