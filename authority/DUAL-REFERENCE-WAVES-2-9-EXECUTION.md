# OLUK Dual-Reference Waves 2–9 Execution

## Purpose

This is the execution order for bringing every entry in `SITE-ROUTE-LEDGER.json` to the customer-facing quality of the approved Your Stack reference. It is deliberately a design programme: a route may become `dual-reference-ready` while its runtime status remains `presentation-only` or `authority-deferred`.

## Non-negotiable acceptance law

Every completed section must have:

1. A canonical Sites implementation, using the Page 09 component graph, governed tokens, approved fonts and registered product media.
2. A native Final-Design mirror at 1440 and 390. The mirror uses Page 09 instances; it is never a page screenshot, Make artifact or copied local card anatomy.
3. A `DESIGN-SYNC-REGISTRY` record with Sites commit, both capture hashes, both Figma node IDs, component provenance and a declared mobile strategy.
4. Customer copy that leads with an outcome and a clear product or experience benefit. Customer surfaces never expose implementation vocabulary.
5. A compact mobile composition: reorder, collapse, summary, carousel or authorised horizontal scroll—not a narrowed desktop page.

Figma Make is used only to explore unresolved information hierarchy. Any relationship selected from a Make run is rebuilt in Sites first and then re-created natively in Final-Design.
