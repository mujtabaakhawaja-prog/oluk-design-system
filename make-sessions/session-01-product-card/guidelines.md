# MF-01A Material Relationship Guidelines

## Why this is the first laboratory

ProductCommerceCard is the smallest customer-facing composition that exercises the unresolved light-mode material relationships together: page canvas, bounded media atmosphere, raised commerce plane, embedded divider or authored seam, product grounding, elevation, spacing, quantified metrics, commerce state and actions.

MF-01A explores those relationships without asking Make to design complete routes or formalize a system. Its outputs remain architectural hypotheses until MF-01B proves transfer into homepage, PDP and evidence contexts.

The controlling adaptive relationship model is [`../../authority/surface-contract.md`](../../authority/surface-contract.md).

## Design objective

Design one proprietary adaptive ProductCommerceCard family across vertical ProductGrid, compact rail/QuickAdd, horizontal related/upsell and derived Featured contexts. The three supplied PNGs are context proofs, not templates.

## Fixed truth

`SARM SERIES · MK-2866 · Ostarine · SKU 80529-01 · 15 MG · 90 SERVINGS · >99% · £43`

## Fixed relationships

- Cool luminous canvas remains visible around independent card objects.
- The bounded white-to-ice media chamber owns product atmosphere.
- The purchase/content plane is white and optically raised.
- Vertical chamber and purchase content connect through a transparent embedded divider.
- Horizontal chamber and purchase content connect through an authored seam.
- ProductMetricRail remains quantified truth and visually outranks qualitative attributes.
- No cobalt outer outline or decorative top-edge eyebrow appears on ProductCommerceCard.
- No cream, broad inverse, blanket grey, card soup or technical-OS decoration.
- No runtime, production-component, token-publication or system-board work.

## Canvas decision logic

- Inside one ProductCommerceCard, use an embedded divider or authored seam—not exposed page canvas.
- Around independent cards or rails, canvas may act as margin and controlled rhythm.
- Do not add a white wrapper around an already raised white card.
- If a gap does not improve reading order, containment or hierarchy, it is deadspacing and should be removed.

## Information sequence

`media chamber → product identity → quantified metrics → quantity or inventory + price → actions`

- Vertical and horizontal seeds use quantity steppers.
- Compact seed uses text-only inventory.
- No crossed price, decimal price, per-serving price, filled qualitative chips or invented evidence.

## Open creative questions

Make may redesign:

- silhouette and corner relationships;
- seam and embedded-divider geometry;
- spacing rhythm and information density;
- chamber proportion and product scale;
- product contact grounding and elevation character;
- metric-cell and qualitative-chip geometry while preserving their semantic distinction;
- action placement and family adaptation.

The three directions must be architectural alternatives—not palette swaps or one rectangle resized four times. Exact spacing, radius, stroke and shadow values are candidates, not promoted rules.

## Typography

- Plus Jakarta Sans: editorial/display.
- Inter: commerce and UI.
- Customer body: 15–16 px minimum.
- Metadata/eyebrows: 12 px minimum.
- Rejected: Barlow Condensed, Archivo, Cousine, Inter Variable.

## Exit

MF-01A ends after three materially different customer-facing hypotheses are exported and a human relationship selection is recorded. Do not synthesize a champion or introduce native nodes inside this run. The selected relationships proceed to MF-01B.
