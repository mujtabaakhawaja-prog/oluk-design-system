# MF-01 Native Light Directional Baseline

This file summarizes the identity relationships MF-01 may creatively enhance. It is not a token sheet or a limit on Make's visual frontier.

## Identity thesis

A cool luminous environment carrying cobalt product objects, calm raised white commerce planes and precise quantified truth. The bottle is the chromatic anchor; the interface borrows cobalt selectively.

## Surface jobs

- **Canvas:** cool blue-white environmental field visible around modules.
- **Media chamber:** bounded white-to-ice product environment with contact grounding.
- **Purchase plane:** raised white decision surface.
- **Evidence:** progressively denser structured information, never a dashboard shell.

## ProductCommerceCard exception map

- The PDP Section 1 full-field rail is not the ProductCommerceCard chamber.
- ProductCommerceCard chambers are bounded and unique to the card family.
- The card has no cobalt perimeter or decorative top edge.
- Vertical chamber/content uses an embedded divider.
- Horizontal chamber/content uses a connected seam.
- Compact purchase content remains white and elevated.

## Color and type references

- Identity/metric cobalt: `#0057FF`.
- Interactive cobalt: `#256DFF`.
- Blue-shifted support references: `#F8FAFC`, `#D9DEE8`, `#667085`, `#101114`.
- Plus Jakarta Sans: display/editorial.
- Inter: commerce/UI.

These are directional references. Candidate variables remain unpublished, and MF-01 does not promote tokens.

## Rejected inheritance

- Pure-white page canvas as a universal law.
- Full-width pale blue/grey section bands.
- “No shadows” as an absolute rule.
- Universal cobalt card borders.
- Dark-first or MENT themes.
- Technical typography and dossier decoration outside evidence.
- Runtime vocabulary or implementation structure.
