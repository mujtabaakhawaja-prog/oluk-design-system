## 4. Experience thesis

OLUK R6 is precision-led evidence commerce.

The experience must hold commercial clarity and evidence authority at the same level. It should feel technically exact, product-confident, and source-aware without turning laboratory language into decoration or customer-facing product claims.

The system synthesis is:

### Porcelain field

- White-dominant authored surfaces.
- Calm hierarchy and meaningful negative space.
- Editorial section structure rather than generic card stacking.

### Cobalt atmosphere

- Pale cobalt fields create structural thresholds and context.
- Opaque white islands can overlap or sit within those fields.
- Saturated cobalt remains scarce and semantically owned.

### Ruled evidence

- Evidence and metric density are organized by hairlines, alignment, and source hierarchy.
- Tables, matrices, records, and methods remain legible and provenance-safe.
- Information density is applied where it builds trust; narrative areas retain breathing room.

### Symmetry within asymmetry

- Outer section composition may use 4/8, 5/7, 7/5, or other asymmetric splits.
- Internal columns, rails, tables, and metrics align precisely.
- Asymmetry creates authorship; alignment creates authority.

“Pharmaceutical-grade,” “clinical,” and related phrases are internal visual-register language only. They are not approved customer-facing claims.

## 5. Light foundation

### Semantic surface ledger

| Role | Value | Use |
|---|---:|---|
| Page | `#F7F8FA` | Root page ground and inter-section breathing space |
| Surface | `#FFFFFF` | Header, primary sections, cards, and white islands |
| Surface soft | `#F4F7FC` | Low-emphasis supporting regions |
| Evidence / atmosphere | `#EEF4FF` | Pale cobalt fields and source-safe evidence districts |
| Text primary | `#101114` | Primary copy and values |
| Text secondary | `#667085` | Supporting copy and metadata |
| Border | `#D9DEE8` | Governed hairlines and boundaries |
| Accent | `#0057FF` | Decisive interaction and selected/focus architecture |

“White canvas” means a white-dominant authored experience. It does not collapse Page and Surface into the same token.

### Cobalt law

Solid `#0057FF` may own:

- primary action;
- active or selected navigation;
- focus/selection architecture;
- links;
- short section eyebrows, indexes, analytical traces, or proof markers with meaning.

Large background fields use the atmospheric surface, not saturated cobalt.

For a product card, allow the primary action plus at most one other cobalt moment, and only when that moment carries interaction, navigation, state, or evidence meaning.

Do not:

- wash every card in blue;
- outline every container in cobalt;
- use cobalt as an arbitrary status color;
- repeat full-width saturated blue buttons until the page becomes visually flat.

### Depth and boundary law

- Zero drop shadow in the light Make lane.
- Depth comes from field changes, overlap, tone, whitespace, scale, and governed hairlines.
- Hairlines articulate semantic boundaries; they are not mandatory around every surface.
- “Raised” means an island or panel has a clear optical relationship to its field. It does not imply shadow.
- Pills are reserved for controls or statuses whose role requires a pill. They are not the default shape for every CTA or metric.
- Exact radius values remain provisional until component candidates are reviewed.

### Typography

- The verified current family is Inter.
- Weight, scale, line length, rhythm, and alignment may be explored within the existing family.
- A new display family is not authorized by a Make run.
- Metrics and evidence may use technical or tabular treatment when it improves scanning, but not as decorative pseudo-science.

### Motion and interaction

- Focus-visible treatment must be explicit.
- Reduced motion must remain viable.
- Motion expresses state or hierarchy; it does not swoop, bounce, or simulate spectacle.
- Exact timings are set during component adoption, not primitive exploration.

## 7. Product card semantic contract

The Make lane locks semantic reading order, not one visual shell:

1. `ProductIdentityStack` — compound, alias, series.
2. `ProductMediaChamber` — governed studio render.
3. `ProductMetricRail` — context-appropriate measurements.
4. `PriceDisplay` — visible but subordinate.
5. `ProductCardAction` — decisive purchase or navigation action.
6. `ProductSpecificationRail` — Featured only.

Rules:

- Modules may form an inset, split, island, overlap, or ruled composition.
- The visual and accessibility reading order must remain unambiguous.
- A field never migrates into a different semantic module.
- Contexts may omit optional modules; they do not exchange module meaning.
- The former `EyebrowBadge` and redundant `ClassificationFooter` are not separate slots.
- An index or series marker may live inside `ProductIdentityStack`.
- Price does not become a metric cell.
- Metrics are an integrated ruled system, not floating tags or chips.

### Context matrix

| Context | Required metrics | Optional/forbidden |
|---|---|---|
| Grid | Strength + Servings | No SpecificationRail; purity omitted in Session 01A |
| QuickAdd | Strength + Servings | Condensed; no SpecificationRail |
| Featured | Strength + Servings + Purity | Full SpecificationRail: Class, Form, Formulation, Testing |

Exact dimensions, media ratios, CTA width/shape, cell ratios, and radius values are creative variables until a candidate is selected and stress-tested.

The legacy production card contract currently places Media before Identity and uses different action labels. That contract must be reconciled before code implementation; Phase 0 does not silently mutate it.

## 8. Composition grammar

The reusable composition family is:

- `Field` — full-width contextual ground.
- `Island` — opaque functional surface within or overlapping a field.
- `Rail` — aligned horizontal or vertical information system.
- `Chamber` — governed media container that establishes product scale.
- `NarrativeRail` — purpose, interpretation, and next-step copy.
- `OperationalPanel` — commerce, media, comparison, records, or evidence.
- `EvidenceDistrict` — source-owned dense data surface.

Guidance:

- Alternate fields when the content mode changes; do not force a stripe pattern.
- Consecutive white sections are allowed when hierarchy and transition remain clear.
- Replace undifferentiated box stacking, not every semantic container.
- Commerce-to-evidence transition should feel like a deliberate mode change rather than a decorative divider.
- Evidence geometry may be borrowed from a node without borrowing its unsupported data or chart.

## 12. Make operating model

The target is holistic; the sessions are bounded.

1. Explore one unresolved primitive or relationship.
2. Return neutral candidates.
3. Figma Agent/Codex compare them against Phase 0.
4. Human selects, rejects, or recombines.
5. Stress only selected candidates across context and viewport.
6. Adopt into Figma components/variables after approval.
7. Translate to runtime only after the adoption gate.

Operational rules:

- Use one prepared input frame whenever possible.
- Do not attach raw source boards, ZIP bundles, or prior Make outputs unless a later prompt explicitly requires them.
- Ask for 3–5 materially different candidates based on problem complexity.
- Differences must be spatial, compositional, or behavioral—not cosmetic skins.
- Make does not rank, score, shortlist, or recommend its own work.
- Primitive sessions do not build routes.
- Responsive and state work follows human shortlisting, not initial divergence.
- Every run returns a traceable result receipt and output node ledger.
