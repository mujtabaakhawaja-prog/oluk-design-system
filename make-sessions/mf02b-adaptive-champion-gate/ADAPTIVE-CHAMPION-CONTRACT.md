## Universal family anatomy

The family preserves this reading order where the density permits it:

> bounded media chamber → identity → status → quantified MetricRail → qualitative information → price/quantity → actions

### Material and edge laws

- The page canvas surrounds independent objects. It never becomes an internal gap between one card's chamber and purchase content.
- Vertical, Featured and Compact use a bounded ice media chamber connected to a raised white purchase plane through an authored seam or embedded divider.
- Horizontal uses a connected sculpted seam. Both zones scale as one semantic object.
- PurchasePanel sits on the PDP first-fold atmospheric exception and contains no duplicate media chamber.
- ProductCommerceCard has no cobalt outer perimeter, no cobalt top-edge eyebrow and no decorative continuous cobalt top edge.
- Cobalt follows `quantified metric > specification > qualitative attribute > structural edge`.
- The only authorized strong internal cobalt edge in this family is a meaningful embedded divider or selected/focus state—not a decorative perimeter.
- Every grid child uses `min-width: 0`; responsive tracks use `minmax(0,...)` or intrinsic constraints.
- `overflow-x: clip` cannot hide a failed specimen. Child bounds must remain inside the intended parent.

### Status laws

- `InventoryStatus` is a green dot plus `IN STOCK` in `#15803d`; a cobalt stock dot is rejected.
- The normalized shared InventoryStatus component set is [`641:17`](https://www.figma.com/design/BEPMuUt1HroEw8xjz8CVyN/Final-Design?node-id=641-17), with `IN STOCK` [`641:8`](https://www.figma.com/design/BEPMuUt1HroEw8xjz8CVyN/Final-Design?node-id=641-8), `OUT OF STOCK` [`641:11`](https://www.figma.com/design/BEPMuUt1HroEw8xjz8CVyN/Final-Design?node-id=641-11) and `UNAVAILABLE` [`641:14`](https://www.figma.com/design/BEPMuUt1HroEw8xjz8CVyN/Final-Design?node-id=641-14) variants.
- `EvidenceStatus` uses the atom relationship from [`518:13092`](https://www.figma.com/design/BEPMuUt1HroEw8xjz8CVyN/Final-Design?node-id=518-13092) and the exact label `OPENLAB VERIFIED`.
- Candidate instances must keep `518:13092` as their main component; locally redrawing the atom is rejected.
- Compact puts InventoryStatus and EvidenceStatus on one status row.
- Inventory and evidence specimens are presentation states only.

### MetricRail laws

- MetricRail is a joined three-cell quantified object, not three generic pills or bare text columns.
- Value leads; label supports.
- The required MK-2866 sequence is `15 MG / STRENGTH`, `90 / SERVINGS`, `>99% / PURITY`.
- Internal dividers are structural and weaker than the quantified values.

### QualitativeChip laws

- Qualitative chips use an icon, secondary field label and primary value.
- Labels are muted and values are stronger; the customer reads the value first.
- Vertical, Featured and PurchasePanel use four qualitative chips.
- Compact omits the four-chip block.
- Horizontal uses one intentional qualitative signal beside its MetricRail, not the full four-chip grid.
- A bare label cannot stand in for an icon-bearing chip.
