## Rules for every session

1. Use the minimum prepared input frame(s); do not attach raw source boards.
2. Keep product, price, claims, and evidence source-safe.
3. Generate 3–5 materially different spatial/compositional hypotheses.
4. Do not accept cosmetic skins of one layout.
5. Do not ask Make to score, rank, shortlist, or name a winner.
6. Stop for human review before expanding contexts, states, or viewports.
7. Primitive sessions do not build routes.
8. Light mode only.
9. Zero drop shadow.
10. Saturated cobalt remains scarce and semantically owned.
11. Return a Make result receipt and exact output node ledger.
12. Preserve rejected candidates until the human decision is recorded.
