The copy sidecar runs one wave ahead of Figma Make. GitHub is the machine-readable mirror; the Figma page is what Make consumes.

Every copy row records:

```text
route
section
copy slot
current copy
```

Figma Make may arrange approved copy, choose line breaks and test it responsively. It must not rewrite approved product facts, analytical claims, actions or route terminology.

## C0 — Universal content system

Start C0 while G0 runs.

Use a voice that is precise, direct and technically credible without sounding like infrastructure documentation. Every opening composition must answer:

1. Where am I?
2. What can I do here?
3. Why should I trust what I am seeing?
4. What should I do next?


## PDP real-data boundary

The PDP copy deck includes compound, alias, class, strength, quantity, purity, price, availability, PurchasePanel, Product Facts, Product Composition, Lab Records, Dossier, Related Products and mobile sticky purchase.

Apply MK-2866 first. Verify MENT, RAD-140 and MK-677. Make must not generate product descriptions, prices, purity values, analytical claims or report data.

## Copy acceptance

Before route Build:

- Every major section has selected or explicitly candidate copy.
- Eyebrows provide context rather than repeat headlines.
- Headlines state one clear proposition.
- Body copy explains the task or benefit.
- CTAs name the actual destination/action.
- Product/evidence facts resolve to governed sources.
- Light and Dark contain identical copy.
- Copy fits at 390 without lost meaning.
- Loading, unavailable, empty and error states provide a next action.

Recommended limits:

| Slot | Limit |
|---|---:|
| Eyebrow | 20–40 characters |
| Route headline | 4–10 words |
| Hero body | 12–28 words |
| Section headline | 3–9 words |
| Section body | 12–30 words |
| Card description | 10–22 words |
| CTA | 2–4 words |
| Empty-state headline | 3–8 words |
| Empty-state body | 10–24 words |

Each promoted Make result returns a text map containing copy ID, route, section, slot, approved string, responsive behavior, destination and truth source. Human promotion approves the visual node and copy map together. Codex implements those exact strings.
