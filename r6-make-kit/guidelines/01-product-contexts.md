# R6 Product Contexts

## Product Identity Hierarchy (fixed)
Compound → Alias → Metrics → Price → Classification → CTA

Never reorder. Never omit Compound or Alias.

## Deprecated Patterns
- No EyebrowBadge in Grid or QuickAdd
- No ClassificationFooter in Grid or QuickAdd
- No SpecRail in Grid or QuickAdd

## Cobalt Ownership Rules
- Cobalt/blue is reserved for interactive affordance only
- Primary CTA gets cobalt
- Secondary actions get surface-variant
- Links get cobalt at rest, darker on hover
- Never use cobalt for decoration, background, or status
